# MYSLEF minder prep example

Derived storyboard for script shots 03–06. Aspect ratio is 9:16. Fields are
optional on other boards. The frames carry the EP-01 camera map: a 35 mm
push-in on 03 and 06, a handheld tilt-down on 04, an 85 mm locked ECU on 05,
and a cut on the bass peak plus a 30 degree angle change into 06. Keyframe
generates each shot for at least 3 seconds, then the cut list trims back to
the planned beats. The plan also sets
`audio_strategy: muted_native`, `budget_cap_credits: 400`, myslef
`voice.speaks: false`, the minder library voice, and the cracked-optic
asymmetry line. The commands are in `docs/RELEASE-NOTES-v0.21.md`. The v0.23 compiled prompts omit the red-chest lock. `docs/JEV-PRE-SPEND-GATE.md` records how the pre-spend gate fails those prompts and passes the hand-fixed ones.
