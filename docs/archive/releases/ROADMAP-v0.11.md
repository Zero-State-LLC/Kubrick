# Kubrick v0.11 Production-Hardening Roadmap

## Objective

Convert Kubrick from a high-signal symbolic reference and prompt-engineering skill into a deterministic, auditable symbolic compiler without weakening its observed-first, latent-operation, provenance, mutation, or fail-closed guardrails.

## Execution waves

### Wave 1: Executable substrate

- Ledger-aware retrieval scoring and reason-vector diagnostics.
- First-class collision prefilters and saturation/debt penalties.
- Receipt caching keyed by brief plus ledger snapshot.
- Pattern-gap reporting.
- Executable motif-graph intermediate representation and validation.
- Initial corpus-density expansion with high-transfer patterns.
- Adversarial and regression fixtures.

### Wave 2: Feedback and production adapters

- Forge outcome extraction from ledger diffs, revisions, collision reports, and ingestion results.
- Multi-signal pattern evolution and retirement proposals.
- Project symbolic ledgers as persistent first-class inputs.
- Model adapters for Grok Imagine, Flux, SD3, and Midjourney.
- Shared graph and residue tracking for storyboard consistency.

### Wave 3: Ecosystem surface

- MCP operators for ledger-aware retrieval, mutation, saturation scoring, graph export, and symbolic-architecture export.
- Closed-loop image evaluation against original symbolic architecture.
- Cross-project performance analytics and payoff-risk modeling.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Weak semantic overlap, excessive saturation, cultural conflict, prohibitions, or unaffordable production cost produce `NOT_COMPUTABLE` with explicit reasons.
2. Every selected pattern is schema-normalized, provenance-linked, mutation-capable, and checked for collisions.
3. Every graph has disentangled layout, semantic, and attribute layers; one or two convergence sites; and no unlicensed attribute leakage.
4. Corpus growth requires a sidecar, provenance, transferable-structure analysis, misuse risks, production cost, and at least one eval fixture.
5. Kubrick remains proposal-generating; Continuity Forge remains canonical authority.
